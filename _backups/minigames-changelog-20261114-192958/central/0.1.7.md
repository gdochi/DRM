### 0.1.7 Change Log
#### Global
##### Added
* Added toy Russian Roulette with solo cash-out, six-seat multiplayer scenes, and player-skin self-aim animations.
* Added solo Snake with a craftable arcade cabinet, Arrow/WASD controls, and configurable rules.
##### Fixed
* Fixed oversized Russian Roulette blocks and expensive selection outlines.
* Fixed Snake food effects appearing at the center instead of the eaten apple.
* Fixed slot machine levers opening the GUI instead of starting a round.
##### Improved
* Improved models.
* Improved Snake movement pacing and input response.
* Improved Snake readability with larger countdown digits and fewer overlapping labels.
* Improved Russian Roulette layout consistency between entry and gameplay.
