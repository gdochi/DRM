## Acquisition guide moved

See [MWENT acquisition settings](#mwent/acquisition) for merchant trades, generated chests, card pools, profile storage and Apply.

Open this screen through **Edit MWENT** in Minigames settings. Enable acquisition, then choose **Merchant trades / Chest rewards / Product pools**. The linked guide explains profile saving versus the topbar Apply.
