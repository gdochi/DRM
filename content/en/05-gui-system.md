## Role

GUI files define what the player sees. Dialogue and shop runtimes decide what the screen does.

1. Pick the GUI type.
2. Set canvas size and background.
3. Place components.
4. Leave runtime-filled regions for choices or products.
5. Export and link it from dialogue or shop data.
