## Choose a faction

A deck combines its chosen faction with neutral cards. There are four basic presets per faction, 24 total, and three leader choices per faction. The core strategies below were already present in the 0.1.1 upload. See [0.1.5 changes](#mwent/version-0-1-5) for new scenarios, locations, traps and siege cards.

| Faction | Main synergies | Counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volkar Empire | Put Order units between armored allies. Crew, Resupply and Rearm repeat Orders while protecting war machines. | Lock key Orders and break adjacent formations. |
| Avalin | Play different categories for Harmony; prepare Symbiosis before playing Nature. Healing, Vitality, Nurture and Grace develop allies. | Control growth engines early and pressure short rounds. |
| Nevra | Place spies in empty enemy sockets and connect espionage, cipher networks and control. A spy on the opposing board adds to the opponent's score. | Remove synergy pieces and manage occupied sockets. |
| Mistwake Clans | Combine self-damage, wounded units and healing for Bloodthirst and Berserk. Other decks use resurrection, frost and rain. | Remove units before healing and contest important graveyard cards. |
| Roden League | Earn Supply through Profit and Caravans, spend it on Fee Orders, and use Bounty and Hoard to prepare the next action. | Remove income sources and force spending below Hoard thresholds. |
| Morga Brood | Connect Consume and Deathwish. Harvest remains at exact death sockets and Incubate into adjacent empty sockets. | Lock Deathwish and interrupt access to remains and free sockets. |

## Resources separate from points

Roden **Supply ranges from zero to nine**. It does not add score and carries half its value, rounded down, to the next round. Battlefield Supply is separate from the deck's provision limit.

Morga remains are not units: they have no power and occupy no socket. Real unit destruction creates them; tokens do not generate more remains. Round cleanup removes them.

Avalin's different categories are not merely different card names. Another card in an already represented category does not count as a new one. Volkar adjacency means immediately neighboring **fixed sockets** in the same row.

## Check your first deck

Pair engines with cards meeting their conditions: Supply income with spending, resurrection with graveyard targets, and growth with its triggers. A legal deck can still fail to produce its synergies.

Changing leaders can change the provision limit. See [collections and decks](#mwent/cards-decks) and the [card specification catalog](#mwent/card-catalog).
