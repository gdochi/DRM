## Global
### Added
* Added faction and mechanic-specific MWENT card sound effects with CC0 source credits.
* Added seven siege engine models with loading and firing animations.
* Added card creator options for the new mechanics, effect costs, and siege commands.
* Added saved opening tactic choices in the deck builder.
* Added configurable MWENT player, player-versus-AI, and AI-versus-AI table matches.
* Added optional spectator cameras and hand visibility settings.
* Added commands to start or cancel AI table matches and enter spectator view.
* Added spectator item betting with refunds and persistent settlements.
* Added craftable roulette machines with weighted prizes, direct right-click play, and in-world prize effects.
* Added shared reward conditions, selection policies, and persistent pending rewards across minigames.
* Added optional Cobblemon Pokemon prizes with party and PC delivery.
### Fixed
* Fixed DNPC Gecko cards showing default appearances instead of their selected models.
* Fixed misplaced deck selection hitboxes and controls overlapping the selected card list.
* Fixed modified card states being lost during draws, revivals, or AI predictions.
* Fixed hidden trap identities leaking to opponents and spectators.
* Fixed Ender Dragon battlefield models appearing smaller than ordinary troops.
### Improved
* Improved MWENT audio mixing by suppressing overlapping voices and repeated area impacts.
* Improved spinning slot performance.
* Improved MWENT betting amount field sizing.
## Forge 1.20.1
### Added
* Added optional siege engine palettes using compatible Create and Big Cannons blocks.
