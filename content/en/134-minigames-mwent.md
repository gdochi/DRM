## Start playing

1. Use `/minigame play mob_battle` or the AI section of an MWENT table.
2. Start with **Learn by Playing**. Practice uses vanilla training cards, requires no owned collection and has no entry fees, rewards or time limit.
3. Practice placement, ending turns, orders, leader abilities, passing and round transitions.
4. Use a ready-made deck in normal matches or save decks from collected cards.

Lessons can be selected freely. Continue Learning opens the first unfinished lesson; interrupted lessons restart their practice state. Previous / Next revisits explanations without rewinding the real cards or score.

## Match flow

Choose among six factions and leader abilities. Place units in fixed front/back-row sockets, combine neighboring cards, and use orders, armor, shields, poison, bleeding, healing, resurrection and special cards. Win two rounds; choosing when to pass preserves resources for later rounds.

Right-click a card to inspect it. Rotate and zoom the 3D battlefield or use card-display mode. Display preferences do not alter server-side rules or scoring.

## Collections, decks and packs

Collect regular and animated physical cards and organize them through the vault and encyclopedia. The pack editor supports fixed contents, weighted pools, quantities, final card choices and appearance. Creators can configure opponent factions / decks, AI difficulty, field themes, music, effects and custom cards.

The game type ID is `mob_battle`. The server owns rules, cards, packs and results. See [tables](#dochi-minigames/minigames-tables) and [files](#dochi-minigames/minigames-files-gui).

## Acquisition and battle music

Distribute physical cards/packs through [merchant and chest rewards](#dochi-minigames/minigames-acquisition). Receiving an item is separate from depositing it in card storage.

Battle music follows the player's deck faction from the coin toss and mulligan onward. Play/Stop and volume controls sit left of Rulebook. Silent 0% retains playback position; playing after Stop restarts the track. Preferences are local and do not edit server JSON or global Minecraft volume. Stopping battle BGM does not allow vanilla background music to take over during the battle.
