## Global
### Added
* Added biome and structure ID/tag filters with inclusion and exclusion lists.
* Added shared guard squad composition and equipment across all supported loaders.
* Added persistent per-structure squad limits and respawn delays.
### Fixed
* Fixed nested location pickers showing parent controls and editor labels through their panels.
* Fixed oversized advanced diagnostics and kept long results inside a scrollable viewport.
### Changed
* Changed guard equipment clearing to standalone switches beside item selectors.
* Changed spawn placement choices to inline dropdowns.
### Improved
* Improved guard flyouts with section dividers and visible structure linkage guidance.
* Improved guard forms with compact actions, aligned fields, and readable selection summaries.
## Fabric & NeoForge 26.3
### Added
* Added saved DNPC clone templates to natural spawn rules.
